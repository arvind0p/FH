One platform to grow through franchising

Franchise Horizon (earlier Franchise Nations) began in 2020 as a unified platform for consultants from across India. Today we help companies expand through franchisees and other methods.

We build the business model, create the brand identity, market the brand, find investors and close the deal. For investors, we profile your goals and match you with suitable business ventures.
