About Franchise Horizon

One platform for franchise and business growth

Franchise Horizon, earlier known as Franchise Nations, was formed in 2020 by bringing together members from different parts of the country to create a unified platform for offering their services. Today, the company has a presence in more than 35 cities across India and 12 countries.

We help companies and brands expand through franchisees and other expansion methods. Our work includes developing business models, brand marketing and promotion, creating brand identities, identifying potential investors and facilitating deal finalisation. We also help prospective investors identify suitable business opportunities through investor profiling.
