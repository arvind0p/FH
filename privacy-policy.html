Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and safeguard the information you share with us.

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Franchise Horizon (“we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, or interact with our advertisements and lead forms.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Enquiry details such as your city, business interests, franchise preferences, investment range and preferred time to connect, where you choose to provide them.
  • Information you voluntarily share through messages, calls, forms or other communications.
  • Basic technical information about website use, such as browser, device and pages visited, where collected through cookies or similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to respond to enquiries, provide information about franchise and business opportunities, understand your interests, connect you with relevant brands or business representatives, follow up through the contact channels you provide, improve our website and enquiry process, prevent misuse, maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Lead Forms and Advertising Platforms

If you submit a lead form on a platform such as Facebook or Instagram, the information you provide may be collected by that platform and shared with us according to its own terms and privacy policy. We use the submitted information to respond to your enquiry and provide relevant follow-up. Please review the privacy settings and policies of the platform you use.

4. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information, when necessary, with:

  • Brands, franchise providers or business representatives when sharing is relevant to your enquiry or connection request.
  • Service providers who help us operate our website, communications or business processes and who are expected to protect the information.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, legal process or to protect rights and safety.

Where practical, we share only the information needed for the specific purpose.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, manage follow-ups, maintain business records and meet legal requirements. We may delete or securely dispose of information when it is no longer required.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, no website, online service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand website traffic and improve user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have shared with us, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. You may also ask us to stop contacting you about an enquiry.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website or communications may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and enquiry services are intended for adults interested in business or franchise opportunities. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
